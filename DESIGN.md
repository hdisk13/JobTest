# Visual brief

Modern, fun, phone-first for young people. Dark shell #0B1220, paper cards #F4F0E6, hot teal #12D4C4, lime CTA #C8F542, ink #0B1220, muted #8B93A7. Chunky grotesque wordmark (Clash Display / Cabinet Grotesk); clean grotesque UI. Take-test is one calm item on a dark shell; results are a screenshot-worthy character sheet with badge/orb scores and punchy Job Zone cards. 24-segment progress pips light teal then lime. No purple SaaS, confetti, cringe slang, noisy questions, or DOL seal as the mark.
