Rules:

- Administer Parts 1–6 in that order (power before speed).
- Official AP is **one sitting**, with a 5-minute break after Part 3 (AM VI.18). It is not two sessions.

- Instruction overhead is about 5–10 minutes per part.

## Scoring

Pipeline:

1. Raw scores.
2. Official guessing penalties on Parts 4–6.
3. Equate Parts 1–6 to GATB Form A converted scores (mean 100, SD 20) via Segall & Monzon 1995.
4. Convert to percentiles 1–99 vs the working-adult sample (50 = average).
5. The examinee sees **percentiles**, not GATB scores.

**Landmine:** keys, equating, and percentile tables are **not** in the archive PDFs. They live in `AP-SP.msi`, Segall & Monzon 1995, and Silva 2002. Do not guess a key.

## Occupation / Job Zone

This is not a cutoff matcher.

- Pearson *r* of the person’s 6-score profile vs each occupation’s OAP (shape, not level).
- Ties: Euclidean distance.
- Positive *r* only.
- Strength labels: Very Strong *p* < .05 / Strong *p* < .10 / fill with Good.
- Max 25 occupations per zone; target ≥ 10.
- 2010 cutoffs for 6 scores: Strong .608, Very Strong .729.
- Prefer 2010 + SPUG labels over UG Chapter 5’s 1999 “min 10 regardless of *r*.”

Job Zone is **not** assigned from scores. Show five lists. Youth picks **Current** vs **Future** from the education / experience / training definitions.

Interests / Holland are a separate tool. Do not invent a new matcher.
